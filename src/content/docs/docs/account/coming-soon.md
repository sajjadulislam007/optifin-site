These features are planned after launch and are **not available yet**:

- **Meta Ads and Google Ads**: ad spend imported and set against Shopify profit, with ROAS next to POAS. Until then, add ad spend as a custom cost.
- **Slack digest**: the profit digest posted to a Slack channel you choose.
- **Google Sheets sync**: a saved report kept in sync with a spreadsheet.
- **TikTok Ads and more ad platforms**: TikTok, Microsoft, Pinterest and Snapchat ad spend set against profit.
- **Custom-metric upgrades**: choose whether higher is better or worse, and insert metrics into formulas.

See the [roadmap page](/coming-soon/) to be notified.
