Product costs (cost of goods sold, or COGS) are usually your largest cost. Without them, profit is overstated, so Optifin makes this the first setup step.

## Three ways to add costs

1. **Shopify cost per item.** If you already fill in *Cost per item* on your Shopify products, Optifin uses it automatically.
2. **CSV import.** In **Costs → Product costs**, download the template, fill in costs by SKU or variant, and import. You preview the changes before they apply.
3. **Manual entry.** Edit any product or variant directly in **Costs → Product costs**.

## Costs change over time

Every cost has an **effective date**. When a supplier raises prices, add the new cost from that date; older orders keep using the cost that applied when they were sold.

## Check your coverage

The dashboard shows how much of your sold units have a cost, for example **Based on 93% of product costs**, with an **Add missing costs** link. Products without a cost are listed in **Costs → Product costs** and in the **Action Center**, so you can fix the ones that matter most first. A missing cost is never counted as $0.
