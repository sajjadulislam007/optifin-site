| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Free | 100 eligible orders per billing cycle, 90 days of history |
| **Growth** | $19.99/month base | 500 orders, then $0.02 per order up to 2,000; maximum $49.99 |
| **Scale** | $49.99/month base | 2,000 orders, then $0.01 per order up to 10,000; maximum $129.99 |

Billing runs through Shopify and appears on your Shopify invoice. Change plans in **Settings → Billing**. To cancel, uninstall Optifin from your Shopify admin; access ends immediately.

## No surprise bills

- Every paid plan has a hard maximum per billing cycle. Shopify does not let Optifin charge above it.
- There is no free trial that turns into a charge. You pay only for the plan you choose.
- When you reach 80% of your included orders, Optifin tells you on the dashboard and in **Settings → Billing**, with the extra cost per order and your maximum bill, before any extra charge starts.
- **Settings → Billing** always shows your bill so far this cycle, the maximum and when the count resets.
- Uninstalling cancels the plan, and Shopify stops all Optifin charges.
