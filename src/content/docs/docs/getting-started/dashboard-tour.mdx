The dashboard answers four questions, top to bottom.

<Image src={dashboardOverview} alt="The Optifin dashboard with key performance cards" widths={[800, 1200, 1600]} sizes="(max-width: 900px) 100vw, 46rem" />

<p class="sample-note">Shown with sample data.</p>


1. **Period and view.** Choose the date range, the comparison (previous period, previous month, previous year or custom) and which saved view to show.
2. **Store setup & profit readiness.** Appears while inputs are missing and links to the fix.
3. **Key performance.** The first six metrics of your view: by default net profit, revenue, net margin, orders, average order value and contribution profit.
4. **Your metrics.** Every other metric in your view, in the same card style.
5. **Executive briefing.** What changed and what needs attention.
6. **Performance trend.** Up to four metrics over time.
7. **Costs and store activity.** Where the money went, plus order and customer summaries.

Each card shows the value, the change against the comparison period (or a short note when it is paused), a sparkline, and a coverage note at the bottom when an input is incomplete.
