Custom costs cover everything that is not a product, shipping or payment cost: app subscriptions, salaries, rent, packaging, agency fees.

## Two behaviors

- **Operating** costs are fixed costs for the business. They reduce net profit.
- **Per-order (variable)** costs apply to each matching order. They reduce contribution profit.

## Schedule and conditions

Costs can be one-off or recurring, start and end on set dates, and apply only to orders that match conditions such as country, sales channel or order value.

Every change is kept in the cost's history.
