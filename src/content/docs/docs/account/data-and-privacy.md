- Optifin has **read-only** access to your store.
- Each store's data is kept separate.
- Optifin does not intentionally store customer names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses. Visitor and click identifiers are stored only as one-way hashes.
- When you uninstall, access ends immediately, and Optifin deletes your store's data when Shopify sends its deletion request 48 hours later.
- Shopify's customer and shop redaction requests are honoured.

See the full [Privacy policy](/privacy/) and [Security and data](/security/).
