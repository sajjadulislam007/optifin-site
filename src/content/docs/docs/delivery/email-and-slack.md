The profit digest is built for your phone: your **net profit** in large type with its change from the period before, one line on the biggest change, and **one thing to fix** (if any) with a button. Revenue, contribution profit and net margin follow in a short list.

## Create a digest

1. Go to **Reports → Report delivery** and choose **Create digest**.
2. Pick how often: weekly or monthly on Starter, and daily, weekly or monthly on Growth and Scale. Then choose the hour, in your store's time zone.
3. Add recipients and choose what to include: comparisons, data-reliability notes and recommended actions.

Use **Send now** to see one straight away.

## Slack

Posting the digest to Slack is [coming soon](/docs/account/coming-soon/).

## Incomplete data

The first line of every digest says whether the period's data is complete: all orders, product costs, shipping, payment fees and ad spend, or exactly what is still missing.

- If orders or ad spend for the period are still arriving, the digest waits (up to 4 hours) so you get finished numbers. If it still isn't complete, it is sent with a note that the figures may change. **Send now** never waits.
- If some costs are missing, profit is shown with its likely range (for example "Likely $4,180 to $4,712 once missing product costs are added"), never as a final figure.

## Paused digests

If nobody opens Optifin for 21 days, the digest pauses itself and sends one email with a link to resume it. Your data keeps updating while it is paused.
