"Trusted profit" means every input your net profit depends on is present: synced orders, product costs, shipping, payment fees and the operating costs you choose to include.

## The readiness checklist

Open **Data health → Readiness**. Each step shows its state and a direct link to fix it:

1. **Shopify data synced** for the period you are looking at.
2. **Product costs** for the products you sell.
3. **Shipping costs**, from rules or your actual labels.
4. **Payment fees** for each payment gateway.
5. **Operating costs** such as apps, salaries or rent (optional, but needed for true net profit).

## On the dashboard

Until setup is complete, the dashboard's **Store setup & profit readiness** card shows what is missing and links to the next step. Numbers that depend on a missing input carry a badge.
