# 01. Brand and Messaging

## Who it is for

**Primary:** Shopify merchants from about 50 to 10,000 orders a month who run
paid ads and want to know their real profit, not just revenue.

**Secondary:** the finance person or agency who reconciles the numbers for
them.

**Their questions, in order:**
1. Am I actually making money?
2. What changed, and why?
3. Where is the money going?
4. What should I fix first?

## The problem we solve

Shopify shows sales. It does not show net profit after product costs, payment
fees, shipping labels, ad spend and operating costs, and it does not tell a
merchant which of those costs are missing.

Competitor apps show a profit number, but their most common 1–3 star reviews
are about numbers that are wrong: fees inflated, ad spend only partly
imported, cost changes that never reach old reports. None of them shows on the
dashboard when an input is missing. (Research: `docs/features-we-need-to-build/04-dashboard-redesign-research.md`,
"Competitive refresh", November 14, 2026.)

## Positioning

> **Optifin shows your true Shopify profit and tells you exactly how
> complete it is.**

The difference is trust: every number shows its coverage ("Costs 93%
covered"), missing inputs are never shown as $0, and a comparison is held back
rather than shown when it would mislead.

## Key messages

1. **Your real profit, not your revenue.** Net profit after product costs,
   fees, shipping, ads and operating costs, in one place.
2. **Numbers you can trust.** Every figure shows what it is based on. Missing
   costs are flagged, never silently filled in.
3. **Know what changed.** The Executive briefing explains the biggest changes
   for the period, in plain language.
4. **Fix what matters.** The Action Center turns problems (missing costs,
   unusual refunds) into a short to-do list.
5. **Built for Shopify merchants.** Installs from the App Store, works inside
   the Shopify admin, no separate login.

## Voice

- Plain, confident, specific. Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives.
- Say what the app does, not what it "empowers" or "unlocks".
- Use the merchant's words: profit, costs, ads, orders. Avoid jargon; when a
  term is needed (contribution margin, POAS, break-even ROAS), explain it once.
- Honest about limits. "Coming soon" means not available yet.

## Taglines (pick one)

- "Your true Shopify profit, with nothing hidden."
- "See real profit. Know it is right."
- "Profit you can trust, inside Shopify."

## Claims rules (must follow)

- **Allowed:** what the released V1 app does; plan prices and limits exactly as
  on the App Store listing; research facts with source and date.
- **Not allowed:** "best", "#1", "first", "only", "most accurate"; invented
  numbers ("save 10 hours a week"); testimonials until real merchants agree to
  be quoted; AI claims; any V1-disabled feature presented as available.
- **Heads-up:** the app's own plan descriptions (`app/data/pricing.js`) list
  advertising providers, Klaviyo audiences, scheduled email delivery and
  alerts. These are switched off in V1 (`PUBLIC_FEATURES`). The website must
  show them as Coming soon, and the in-app plan text should be corrected
  before release so the app, listing and website all agree.
