title: "Currency and accounting settings"
description: "Set your reporting currency, exchange-rate handling and accounting policy in Optifin."
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

- **Currency (Settings → Currency):** Optifin reports in your store currency and converts costs entered in other currencies. A cost in a currency without a rate is flagged rather than guessed.
- **Accounting (Settings → Accounting):** choose policies such as how refunds and shipping charged are treated. Changing a policy recalculates affected figures.
