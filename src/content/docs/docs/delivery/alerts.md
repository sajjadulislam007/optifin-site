Alerts tell you when something changed enough to need attention. You'll find them under **Action Center → Alerts**.

## Two kinds of rules

- **Smart guardrails**, such as Net profit drop or Ad spend up while revenue is down. These alert only when the change passes your threshold, is material, and is unusual compared with at least 6 of 8 comparable past periods. That keeps normal ups and downs out of your inbox.
- **Required protection**, such as Missing COGS, Sync failure and Missing exchange rate. These are always on, on every plan, so incomplete profit is never presented as final.

## What each plan includes

- **Starter:** the essential data-safety alerts with safe defaults.
- **Growth:** adds profit, refund, fee, shipping, promotion and stock alerts, with your own thresholds and minimum impact.
- **Scale:** every alert, with longer baselines, advanced scopes and escalation history.

## Working an alert

Each alert groups related records into one incident with the exact orders or products affected. You can acknowledge it, snooze it or resolve it, and serious ones become cases in the Action Center. Alert digests can be emailed on a schedule from **Reports → Report delivery**.

## Urgent problems arrive right away

You don't have to wait for the next digest when something is seriously wrong. Critical problems, and problems that come back after being fixed (for example a broken Shopify sync), are sent as soon as they are found to the people on your digest.

- At most 3 a day, and never between 10 pm and 7 am in your time zone. Overnight alerts arrive at 7 am.
- Each message names the problem, the profit at stake and one button to fix it.
- A snoozed alert is never sent, and the same alert is not repeated within 24 hours.
- Turn it off per digest with **Send urgent problems right away**.

On days when nothing needs you, the alert digest isn't sent at all.
