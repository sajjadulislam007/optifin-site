Optifin and Shopify can show different numbers for the same period. Usually there is a clear reason.

| Reason | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Costs** | Shopify reports sales; Optifin subtracts product, shipping, fee and operating costs. |
| **Refunds** | Shopify may report a refund on the day it was issued; Optifin follows your accounting policy. |
| **Timezone** | Both use your store timezone, but a report exported in another timezone shifts day boundaries. |
| **Taxes and shipping** | Whether shipping charged and taxes are included in "sales" differs between reports. |
| **Sync timing** | Very recent orders may not have arrived yet; check the Shopify orders line in **Data health**. |

## Check it yourself

**Orders → Investigate differences** compares Optifin's totals with Shopify's for a period and lists the orders behind any difference.
