# 05. Documentation Content

Built with Starlight at `optifin.app/docs`. Goal: a merchant gets 100% out of
Optifin without contacting support. Each guide: what it is, why it matters,
step-by-step with screenshots, what the numbers mean, common problems.

## Structure

### Getting started
1. **Install Optifin** (from the App Store, permissions explained).
2. **Your first sync**: what is imported, how long it takes, the Status page.
3. **Add your product costs**: Shopify cost per item, CSV import, defaults.
4. **Reach trusted profit**: the readiness checklist, step by step.
5. **Tour of the dashboard** (5 minutes).

### Understanding your numbers
- How Optifin calculates net profit (formula, every component).
- Coverage and badges: "Partial", "Estimated", "Costs 93% covered".
- Why a comparison is held back.
- Metric glossary: revenue, net sales, gross profit, contribution profit,
  net profit, margin, AOV, MER, ROAS, POAS, break-even ROAS, CAC, LTV.
- Why Optifin may differ from Shopify reports (timezone, refunds, taxes).

### Dashboard
- Date ranges and comparisons.
- Executive briefing.
- Performance trend.
- Customize: views, revisions, templates, reorder, Your metrics.
- Custom metrics: formulas, formats, examples.
- Targets and pacing.
- Exporting the dashboard.

### Costs
- Product costs (effective dates, suppliers, bulk import, history).
- Shipping costs and rules.
- Payment fees and gateway rules.
- Custom costs (operating vs per-order, recurring, conditional).
- What happens to past orders when a cost changes.

### Orders and products
- Order profitability and the order detail breakdown.
- Product profitability, comparisons and missing-cost flags.
- Bought together and product journeys.
- Reconciliation with Shopify.

### Customers
- LTV, CAC, retention and cohorts.
- Customer segments and custom rules.

### Discounts and returns
- Promotion profitability.
- Returns and refunds.

### Reports
- Profit and Loss report.
- Custom reports, templates and exports.

### Planning and simulator
- Profit simulator walkthroughs (price change, cost change, ad spend).
- Profit plans and actual-versus-plan.

### Action Center and Status
- Working a case from open to verified.
- Status page and sync health.

### Account
- Plans, usage and billing; upgrading, downgrading and cancelling.
- Currency settings and accounting policies.
- Data, privacy and deletion.

### Help
- FAQ.
- Troubleshooting (numbers look wrong, sync is slow, missing products).
- Contact support.

## Writing rules

- Task-first titles ("Add product costs", not "Product cost management").
- One screenshot per key step, annotated; alt text describes the screen.
- Every page ends with "Related" links and "Still stuck? Contact support".
- Coming-soon features are not documented as available; a single "Coming
  soon" page links to the roadmap.
- Each page has a unique title and meta description (SEO), and a "Last
  updated" date.
