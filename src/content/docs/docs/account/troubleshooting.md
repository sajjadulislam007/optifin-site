---

| Problem | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Profit looks too high** | Missing product costs. Check the coverage badge and **Costs → Product costs**. |
| **Numbers differ from Shopify** | See [Why Optifin differs from Shopify](/docs/numbers/differences-from-shopify/) and use **Investigate differences**. |
| **Card says "Comparison after…"** | The period is still syncing or costs are incomplete. See [Why a comparison is paused](/docs/numbers/held-back-comparisons/). |
| **New orders are missing** | Open **Data health** and choose **Refresh now** on the Shopify orders line. |
| **A product is missing** | Refresh Shopify data; archived products still appear in reports for past sales. |
| **Ad spend is $0** | Add ad spend as a custom cost (Costs → Custom costs). Meta Ads and Google Ads connections are [coming soon](/docs/account/coming-soon/). |

Still stuck? Email [support@optifin.app](mailto:support@optifin.app) with your store name and what you see.
