| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Gross sales** | Product prices before discounts and refunds. |
| **Net sales (revenue)** | Gross sales − discounts − refunds. |
| **Gross profit** | Net sales − product costs. |
| **Gross margin** | Gross profit ÷ net sales. |
| **Contribution profit** | Net sales − product, shipping, payment and variable costs. |
| **Net profit** | Contribution profit − advertising − operating costs. |
| **Net margin** | Net profit ÷ net sales. |
| **AOV** | Average order value: net sales ÷ orders. |
| **Average order profit** | Net profit ÷ orders. |
| **MER** | Marketing efficiency ratio: net sales ÷ total ad spend. |
| **ROAS** | Return on ad spend: attributed revenue ÷ ad spend. |
| **POAS** | Profit on ad spend: attributed contribution profit ÷ ad spend. |
| **Break-even ROAS** | The ROAS at which ads stop losing money: 1 ÷ contribution margin before ad spend. |
| **CAC** | Customer acquisition cost: ad spend ÷ new customers. |
| **LTV** | Lifetime value: what a customer brings over time, on revenue or on contribution profit. |
| **Repurchase rate** | Share of customers who ordered more than once. |

Hover the **i** icon on any card in Optifin to see its definition in the app.
