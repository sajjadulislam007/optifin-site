- Filter by **status** and **profitability** (profitable, losing order and more), or search by order number, product or SKU.
- Each row shows revenue, refunds and whether the order made money.
- Open an order to see the full breakdown: revenue, discounts, refunds, product costs, shipping, fees and the resulting profit.
- Custom and draft-order items have no product cost in Shopify. Use **Set cost** on the line to enter one for that order, so the item does not look like pure margin.
- **Export report** downloads the filtered list.
