In **Plan**, create a monthly or quarterly plan from your actual baseline, with base, best and downside scenarios. Lock a plan to keep it as a fixed reference, then track **actual versus plan** as the period runs. Locked monthly plans can publish targets to your dashboard. Planning is available on Growth and Scale.
