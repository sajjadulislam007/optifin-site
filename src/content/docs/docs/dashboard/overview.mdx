## Date range and comparison

Choose a preset (today, last 7 or 30 days, this month, last quarter and more) or a custom range. Pick a comparison: previous period, previous month, previous year or a custom period.

## Today so far

The row at the top shows revenue, orders, AOV and gross profit for today so far, compared with yesterday **up to the same time**, so half a day is never set against a whole one. **View today's orders** opens them in Orders.

## Reading a card

- **Value** for the selected range.
- **Change** against the comparison, coloured by whether higher is good for that metric.
- **"vs" value** from the comparison period.
- **Sparkline** of the trend across the range.
- **Coverage note** at the bottom when an input is incomplete, for example "Based on 93% of product costs", with a link to finish it.
- **Click the card** to open the orders or products behind the number, on the same dates and sorted by what drives it.
- **…** menu to open those orders or products, or add the metric to the Performance trend.

## Export

Use **Export** in the dashboard header to download the current view as CSV, Excel or PDF (formats depend on your plan).
