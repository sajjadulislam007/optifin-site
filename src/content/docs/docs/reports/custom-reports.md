In **Reports → Report templates**, start from a ready-made report, or choose **Build your own**: choose metrics, dimensions and filters, preview the result and save it. Saved reports can be archived and exported. Limits and formats depend on your plan (Starter: one saved report with CSV; Growth: 10 reports with CSV, Excel and PDF; Scale: 50 with revision history).
