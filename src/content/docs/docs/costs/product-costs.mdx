## Effective-dated costs

Each cost applies from its effective date. Add a new cost when a supplier changes price; earlier orders keep the old cost.

## Import

Download the CSV template, fill it in and import. You see a preview of every change before it is applied, and each import is kept in history.

## Filters

Filter by **cost state** (missing, confirmed and more) to find products that still need a cost. Sort by revenue to fix the products that matter most first.

## Landed costs in bulk

Select several variants and add inbound freight per unit and duty as a percentage of the supplier cost. Other cost components are kept, and you see the new total for each row before you apply it.

## Costs that look wrong

Optifin flags a cost of 0, a placeholder such as 0.01 or 9999, and a cost at or above the selling price. Use the **Looks wrong** filter to review them. Flags never change your numbers. Confirmed costs that have not changed in 12 months are marked too, so you can check they are still current.

## What changes when you edit a cost

Optifin recalculates the affected orders and shows the impact on profit for the period.
