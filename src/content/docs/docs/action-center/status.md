**Data health** answers one question: can you trust your profit right now? Open it from the dashboard's status strip, the Action Center or Settings.

It is a short checklist. Each line is one thing behind your profit, with a plain status and one button to fix it:

- **Shopify orders**: how many orders are imported and when they last updated. You can pause or resume a long import, or refresh now.
- **Product costs**, **shipping costs** and **payment fees**: whether every order has them.
- **Currency conversion**: only shown if you sell in more than one currency.
- **Profit settings**: whether you have confirmed how refunds, order dates and missing costs are treated.
- **Totals match Shopify**: whether Optifin's totals have been compared with Shopify's.
- **Ad spend**: once ad platforms can be connected, whether the imported spend matches what each ad account reports.

Below the list, **Connections** shows each connected ad account and cost source, and flags any that need reconnecting.

Need the details of a past import, export or report delivery? Choose **View update history** at the bottom of the page.

Problems that need you also appear in the **Action Center**.
