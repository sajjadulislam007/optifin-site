After you install, Optifin imports your store history from Shopify: orders, refunds, products, inventory, customers, fulfillments and returns. New orders and changes then arrive automatically through Shopify webhooks.

## How long it takes

Small stores finish in minutes. Stores with many years of orders can take longer; Optifin imports in the background and you can use the app meanwhile.

## While the sync runs

- Pages show what has been imported so far.
- Numbers for a period that is still syncing are marked, and comparisons for that period are **paused** so a half-imported month is never compared with a complete one.

## Check progress

Open **Data health**. The Shopify orders line shows how many orders are imported so far, and the rest of the checklist shows what else your profit still needs.

## Refresh on demand

Use **Refresh Shopify data** on the dashboard menu or in Data health to pull the latest changes immediately.
