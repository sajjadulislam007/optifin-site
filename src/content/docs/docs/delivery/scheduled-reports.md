## Scheduled reports

In **Reports → Report delivery**, schedule the Profit and Loss, a saved custom report or a dashboard view. Optifin emails it as an attachment on your schedule.

| | Starter | Growth | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schedules | 1 (weekly or monthly) | 10 (daily, weekly or monthly) | 25 |
| Recipients per schedule | 1 | 5 | 10 |
| Formats | CSV | CSV, Excel, PDF | CSV, Excel, PDF |

A scheduled custom report always uses the saved revision you picked, so its numbers don't change if someone edits the report later.

## Secure report links (Growth and Scale)

Create a link to a generated report that expires and has a download limit. You can revoke it at any time.
