Optifin is a Shopify app. You install it from the Shopify App Store and use it inside your Shopify admin; there is no separate account or password.

## Steps

1. Open the Optifin listing on the Shopify App Store and select **Install**.
2. Review the permissions. Optifin asks only to **read** your store data (orders, products, inventory, customers, fulfillments and returns). It cannot change your store.
3. Approve. Optifin opens in your Shopify admin under **Apps**.
4. Optifin starts importing your history straight away. You can keep working while it runs.

## Plans

You start on the free **Starter** plan. You can change plans at any time from **Settings → Billing**; billing runs through Shopify. See [Plans and billing](/docs/account/plans-and-billing/).

## What Optifin reads, and why

| Data | Used for |
|---|---|
| Orders, refunds and discounts | Revenue, refunds, discounts and order profit |
| Products, variants and costs per item | Product costs (COGS) and product profit |
| Inventory and locations | Inventory value and stock context |
| Customers | Lifetime value, cohorts and segments |
| Fulfillments and returns | Shipping context and returns profitability |

Optifin does not intentionally store customer names, email addresses or postal addresses. See [Security and data](/security/).
